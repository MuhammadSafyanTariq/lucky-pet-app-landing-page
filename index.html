Features

Built for pets. And the people obsessed with them.

SHARE

Post the moments, not the highlight reel

Photos and videos with captions, likes, comments and shares — updating in real time. Stories cover the quick stuff: the muddy paw, the stolen sock. The feed caches locally, so it loads instantly even when your signal doesn't.

  • Real-time likes, comments and shares
  • Stories for the moments that don't need to last
  • Offline caching — never a blank screen
GO LIVE

Your followers, live and talking back

Start a stream in a tap. Viewers join with live chat and a running viewer count, powered by Agora for low-latency video. Puppy naps, training sessions, grooming days — people show up for all of it.

Search by username or name, follow back, and DM privately when a comment thread turns into a friendship.

GIFTS

Applause you can actually cash

Viewers send virtual gifts during live streams and on posts. Coin packs are bought through secure in-app purchase, so the person sending a gift and the creator receiving it are both covered.

Gifts on live Gifts on posts Secure coin packs

For pet businesses

Advertise fairly, reach real pet lovers

Groomers, trainers, treat makers and small brands can run campaigns inside the app. Placement isn't auctioned to the biggest budget: a fairness algorithm rotates exposure, prioritises accounts people already follow, caps daily reach, and gives under-exposed campaigns their turn.
