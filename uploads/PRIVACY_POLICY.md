# Privacy Policy for Lucky's Pet App

**Effective date:** August 13, 2026

Lucky's Pet App ("Lucky's Pet App," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Lucky's Pet App mobile applications (iOS and Android) and the companion web app (together, the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have.

By creating an account or otherwise using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information you give us directly

- **Account information:** first and last name, email address, phone number, username, password (managed securely by Firebase Authentication — we do not see or store your plain-text password), profile bio, pet name, and pet type.
- **Profile and content you create:** profile photos, posts (photos and videos), captions, comments, stories, direct messages (including text, media, and voice messages where used), and live stream broadcasts.
- **Payout and creator information:** if you request a withdrawal of earnings, we collect the recipient name, contact email, country, payout method, and payout account details you provide (for example, IBAN or other bank/payout information), along with any notes you add to the request.
- **Support communications:** anything you send us when you contact support (e.g., at luckyspetapp@gmail.com).
- **Referral/invite content:** the referral link and message we generate on your behalf when you choose to invite others.

### 1.2 Information collected automatically

- **Activity and interaction data:** posts you like, comment on, or share; accounts you follow, block, or report; users you message; live streams you join or host; virtual gifts you send or receive; your coin balance and coin/gift/purchase transaction history; ads you view or click on.
- **Presence data:** your online/offline status and last-active timestamp, which are visible to people you have an active conversation with.
- **Story view data:** when you view someone's story, your user ID, username, display name, and profile picture are recorded as a "viewer" and are visible to that story's owner.
- **Device and technical data:** device type and identifiers, operating system and app version, crash and debug logs, and your push notification token.
- **Purchase data:** in-app purchase product identifiers, transaction status, and coin-credit records associated with your account. We do not collect or store your full payment card number — purchases are processed directly by the Apple App Store or Google Play.

We do **not** collect your precise (GPS) location, and the App does not request location permissions.

### 1.3 Information from permissions you control

The App will ask for the following device permissions only when a related feature is used, and you can change or revoke them at any time in your device settings:

- **Camera and microphone:** used to broadcast and participate in live streams, and for voice messages in chat, where that feature is used.
- **Photo library:** used to let you choose photos/videos to post or use as your profile picture, and to save media from the App to your device.
- **Contacts:** used only for the "Invite from Contacts" feature. Your contacts are read from your device to show you a picker so you can choose who to invite — **we do not upload, transmit, or store your contact list on our servers.** If you decline or revoke this permission, you can still invite people using the general share/link option.
- **Notifications:** used to send you push notifications about likes, comments, messages, follows, live streams, and other account activity.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

- Create, secure, and manage your account, and authenticate you.
- Operate core features: the feed, stories, comments, likes, follows, direct messaging, and live streaming.
- Process coin purchases, deliver purchased coins, and process virtual gift transactions.
- Review and process withdrawal/payout requests, including fraud and duplicate-payout prevention.
- Operate the in-app advertising platform: review and approve ads, place them in the feed and live list, track impressions/clicks and daily viewing limits, and apply the associated coin mechanics.
- Provide safety tools such as blocking, reporting, and content moderation, and enforce our [Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md) and [Community Guidelines](COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES.md).
- Send you service and activity notifications.
- Maintain, secure, debug, and improve the App.
- Comply with legal obligations, including recordkeeping related to payouts where required by law.

## 3. How We Share Information

**We do not sell your personal information.**

- **Other users:** your public profile, posts, stories, comments, likes, follower/following lists, live broadcasts, and gifts sent/received are visible to other users of the App as designed (unless you have blocked or removed them, or made relevant content private where such an option exists).
- **Service providers:** we share information with vendors who help us operate the App, under obligations to protect it:
  - **Firebase / Google** — authentication, database (Cloud Firestore), file storage, push notifications (Cloud Messaging), and web hosting.
  - **Cloudinary** — hosting, processing, and delivery of images and videos.
  - **Agora** — real-time audio/video infrastructure used to transmit your camera and microphone stream during a live broadcast or live session you join.
  - **Apple App Store / Google Play** — billing and payment processing for coin purchases. We do not receive your full payment details from them.
  - Our backend API (hosted on Vercel), used for functions such as the gift catalog.
- **Advertising:** the App's ads are created by other users/businesses of Lucky's Pet App and shown only within the App. **We do not use third-party ad networks, ad exchanges, or advertising SDKs**, and we do not share your data with outside advertisers.
- **Legal and safety:** we may disclose information if required by law, or if we believe in good faith it is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Lucky's Pet App, our users, or the public, or to investigate fraud or violations of our Terms.
- **Business transfers:** if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Policy (or a policy that provides equivalent protections).

## 4. Data Retention

We retain your account and content data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the App, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, and comply with legal obligations. Financial records related to coin purchases and creator withdrawals may be retained longer than other data where necessary for accounting, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes, even after account deletion.

---

## 5. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from **Settings > Delete Account**. When you do, we also delete:

- All your posts, and the associated media stored in Cloudinary.
- Your follow relationships (followers/following) and related counts.
- Your profile image stored in Cloudinary.
- Your Firebase Authentication account.

Some information may persist for a limited period in backups, or be retained where we are legally required to keep it (for example, payout and transaction records needed for accounting or fraud investigations).

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You can exercise most of these directly in the App:

- **Edit** your profile information at any time.
- **Manage notifications** through your device settings.
- **Revoke the Contacts, Camera, Microphone, or Photo Library permission** at any time in your device settings.
- **Block or unblock** other users.
- **Report** content or accounts you believe violate our rules.
- **Delete your account**, as described above.

For any other request — including requests to access or delete specific data we hold about you — contact us at **luckyspetapp@gmail.com**. We will respond consistent with applicable law.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to, and must not be used by, children under **13 years old**, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it.

Users **between 13 and 17 years old** may use the App only with the involvement, permission, and supervision of a parent or legal guardian, who is responsible for that minor's use of the App and agrees to our [Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md) on the minor's behalf. Certain features carry additional requirements for this age group:

- **In-app purchases** of coins require the consent and involvement of the account holder responsible for the associated app store account (consistent with Apple's and Google's own purchase-authorization controls).
- **Sending or receiving virtual gifts** and **hosting or appearing in live streams** should occur only with active parental awareness and supervision.
- **Withdrawing earnings to a bank or payout account** generally requires the recipient to be an adult (18 years or older, or the age of majority in their jurisdiction), consistent with standard payment-processor and banking requirements. Where a user is a minor, a payout can only be completed to a parent or legal guardian's own verified account, acting on the minor's behalf.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information without your consent, please contact us at **luckyspetapp@gmail.com** so we can address it.
