# Community Guidelines for Lucky's Pet App

**Effective date:** August 13, 2026

Lucky's Pet App exists so pet lovers can share moments, connect with each other, go live, and support the creators they love. That only works if the community feels safe, genuine, and kind. These Guidelines explain what's expected of everyone who posts, comments, messages, streams, or advertises on Lucky's Pet App, and what happens when the rules are broken.

These Guidelines apply everywhere in the App: posts, stories, comments, direct messages, live streams, profiles and usernames, and ads. They work alongside our [Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md) and [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md).

---

## 1. Be Real

- Use your own identity. Don't impersonate another person, pet, brand, or Lucky's Pet App itself.
- One account per person. Don't create multiple accounts to get around a suspension, inflate your follows, or abuse coins, gifts, ads, or withdrawals.
- Don't buy, sell, or trade accounts.

## 2. Be Kind

- No harassment, bullying, threats, or targeted abuse of any user — including in comments, direct messages, and live chat.
- No hate speech or content that attacks people based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or other protected characteristics.
- No sharing someone's private information (address, phone number, financial details, etc.) without their consent.
- No sharing of someone else's private photos or videos without their consent.

## 3. Keep Content Safe

- **No nudity or sexual content**, in posts, stories, messages, or live streams.
- **No graphic violence** or content that glorifies it.
- **Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material or any content that endangers or sexualizes minors.** Violating accounts are permanently banned immediately and may be reported to law enforcement and relevant child-safety authorities.
- **No promotion of self-harm or suicide.** If you or someone you know is struggling, please reach out to a local crisis line or emergency service.
- No content promoting illegal drugs, weapons, or other illegal activity.

## 4. Love Pets, Protect Pets

Lucky's Pet App is built for pet lovers, so we hold pet-related content to a clear standard:

- No content depicting or promoting animal abuse, cruelty, or neglect.
- No promotion of illegal animal trade, poaching, or activities like dog/animal fighting.
- Content about pet care, training, or health is shared by community members based on their own experience — it is not veterinary advice. Always consult a licensed veterinarian for your pet's health.

## 5. Live Streaming Etiquette

Live streams are real-time and not reviewed before they're shown, so extra care applies:

- Everything in Sections 2–4 applies live, exactly as it does to posts.
- Only feature other people on your stream if they've consented to appear.
- Don't stream in a way that invades someone else's privacy (e.g., filming people or private property without consent).
- We may end a live stream immediately, without warning, if it breaks these Guidelines.

## 6. Respect Ownership

- Only post content you made or that you have permission to use.
- Don't repost someone else's photos, videos, or written content as your own.
- Respect copyright and trademark rights — including music, artwork, and brand logos.

## 7. Play Fair with Coins, Gifts, and Ads

- Don't manipulate the gifting system — self-gifting, using extra accounts to send yourself gifts, or gifting with fraudulent payment methods is prohibited and can result in forfeited earnings and account termination.
- Don't run scams, fake fundraisers, or misleading promotions.
- Ads must be honest and comply with the law — no illegal products/services and no misleading claims. All ads are reviewed before going live, and we may reject or remove any ad at any time.
- Don't click-farm or use bots to fake ad engagement.

## 8. Keep It Genuine, Not Spammy

- No spam, repetitive unsolicited messages, or mass unsolicited invites through the referral tool.
- No phishing links, malware, or scam links.
- No unauthorized automated ("bot") activity — including auto-liking, auto-following, or auto-commenting.
- Undisclosed promotional or multi-level-marketing solicitation is not allowed.

## 9. Follow the Law

You're responsible for complying with the laws that apply to you wherever you're located, in addition to these Guidelines.

---

## 10. Reporting

If you see something that breaks these Guidelines, please report it using the report option on the post, profile, or in Settings. You can also block any user at any time — blocking removes them from your feed, search results, and followers/following, and ends any follow relationship between you.

## 11. What Happens When Guidelines Are Broken

Depending on the severity and context of a violation, we may take actions including:

- Removing the content.
- Issuing a warning.
- Restricting specific features (e.g., live streaming, messaging, gifting, or advertising).
- Temporarily suspending the account.
- Permanently banning the account, including forfeiture of coins or earnings connected to the violation.
- Reporting to law enforcement or relevant authorities, where required or appropriate (in particular for content endangering minors).

We aim to be fair and consistent, but we make these calls at our discretion based on the information available to us. If you believe a decision was made in error, you can appeal by contacting **luckyspetapp@gmail.com**.

## 12. Updates to These Guidelines

We may update these Guidelines as our community and features evolve. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised Guidelines.
